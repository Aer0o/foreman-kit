WHAT TO SAY TO CLAUDE
=====================

Drag files into the chat, or tell Claude which folder they're in. Then ask the way you'd ask
a person.

REPORTS AND DOCUMENTS
  "Write a site report for the Main St job from these notes and photos."
  "Turn this into a proper proposal on our letterhead."
  "Make this document look professional."
  "Fix the wording in this Word file. Don't change the numbers."
  "Proofread this before I send it."
  "Make a 5-slide presentation from this report for the owner meeting."

TENDERS AND QUOTES
  "Go through these tender documents and tell me what's easy to miss."
  "Write the RFI questions for what the specs don't say."
  "Check my quote before I send it to the GC."
  "We missed the firestopping on the last job. Add it to the checklist."

PARTS AND SPECS
  "What's the catalogue number for a 20A arc-fault breaker for this panel?"
  "Is this fixture CSA approved? Show me where it says so."

EXCEL
  "Pull the lighting fixture schedule out of this PDF into Excel."
  "Make a chart of material costs by month from this sheet."

EMAILS
  "Write an email to the client chasing the invoice from October."
  "Reply to this GC and say we need a change order for the extra outlets."
  "Learn how I write" (then paste two or three of your own emails).

GOOD HABITS
  - One job per chat. Start a new chat for the next job. Long chats use up your plan faster.
  - If Claude gives a part number or price, it should show where it came from. If it doesn't,
    ask "where did that come from?"
  - Claude saves changed files as "v2" next to your original. Claude leaves your original alone.
