# Document house style

Claude applies this to every document it makes and updates it when he corrects a look.
Brand colour and logo come from profile.md.

- Body font: Aptos 11 pt (fallback Calibri 11 pt). Headings: same font, bold.
- Sizes: title 20 pt, heading 1 14 pt, heading 2 12 pt, body 11 pt, captions and footers 9 pt.
- Line spacing 1.15, 6 pt after paragraphs. Margins 2 cm (letter size paper).
- One accent colour (the brand colour) for headings and table header text. Body text near-black.
- Tables: thin rules, bold header row, numbers right-aligned, no coloured fills on every row.
- Footer: company name left, "Page X of Y" right. First page has the logo top left if there is one.
- Slides: one idea per slide, title says the point, at most 5 short bullets, 24 pt minimum body text.
