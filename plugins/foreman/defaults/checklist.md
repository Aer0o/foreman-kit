# Missed-scope checklist

> Claude walks this list on every tender review. When you report a miss ("we missed X on job Y"),
> Claude adds a line under the right heading with today's date. Claude never deletes or rewords old
> lines unless you ask.

## General conditions & site

- Site supervision: is foreman time carried for the full schedule, including closeout?
- Site office, trailer, storage container, laydown area: provided by the GC or by us?
- Daily cleanup, waste bins and disposal: whose cost?
- Parking, travel time, out-of-town or remote-site costs: carried?
- Site-specific safety requirements beyond the usual (orientations, fall protection plans, safety staff)?
- Lifts, scaffolds and hoisting for electrical work: provided by the GC or by us?
- Security clearances, escorts or background checks (schools, hospitals, secure facilities)?

## Permits, inspections & fees

- ESA notification of work and permit fees: included, and who files?
- Utility connection: who applies to the local distribution company (e.g. Hydro One or the local LDC) and pays its fees?
- Utility lead time for design, offer to connect and energization: does it fit the schedule?
- Utility-required civil work (ducts, pull boxes, transformer pad, metering cabinet): whose scope?
- Temporary power: application, service, metering and the monthly energy bill: who pays?
- Municipal permits for work on roads or sidewalks (road occupancy, excavation): needed, and whose cost?
- Locates before digging (Ontario One Call plus private locates on site): who arranges and pays?

## Temporary works

- Temporary lighting for other trades: to what level, and for how long?
- Temporary power for other trades: how many panels, cords and outlets, and for how long?
- Temporary power for trailers, hoarding lights, heaters, hoists or cranes?
- Keeping existing systems live during construction (temporary feeds, phasing, shutdowns)?
- Keeping fire alarm coverage in occupied areas during work, and fire watch if it goes down?

## Service & distribution

- Service size and utility voltage confirmed; is the transformer supplied by the utility or by us?
- Switchgear and transformer lead times vs the schedule, and who carries storage if they arrive early?
- Concrete housekeeping pads and bollards for electrical equipment: ours or the concrete trade's?
- Grounding and bonding (ground rods or grid, bonding to steel, water and gas): fully carried?
- Short-circuit, coordination and arc flash studies: required, and who does them?
- Arc flash labels, equipment nameplates and lamacoids: included?

## Branch wiring & devices

- Wiring method required by the spec in each area (conduit, armoured cable, non-metallic): matches what we priced?
- Device grade, colour and cover plate material as specified?
- Mounting heights, and devices installed in millwork or furniture: whose scope?
- Firestopping of electrical penetrations: who carries it?
- Coring and sleeves through concrete, and scanning or X-ray before coring: ours or others'?
- Exposed conduit: painting required, and by whom? Surface raceway type specified?
- Seismic restraint of conduit and equipment: required?

## Lighting & controls

- Light fixtures: supplied by us, or owner-supplied and we install?
- Named fixtures with no alternates allowed: priced as named?
- Lighting control system: programming, start-up and manufacturer commissioning included?
- Emergency lighting and exit signs: battery units or central system, and the exit sign type specified?
- Site and exterior lighting: poles, concrete bases, trenching and backfill: whose scope?
- Occupancy sensors, daylight controls and energy-code controls: all counted?

## Fire alarm

- Fire alarm in our scope or a separate contractor? Check every Division 28 section.
- Fire alarm verification by the manufacturer's representative, and the verification certificate: carried?
- Additions to an existing panel: compatibility, reprogramming and the manufacturer's charges?
- Connection to the monitoring company: phone lines or communicator, and who arranges it?
- Interfaces: door hardware, elevators, HVAC shutdown, sprinkler flow and tamper switches: who wires what?
- Voice evacuation, two-way communication or firefighter phones: specified?

## Low voltage / data / security

- Structured cabling in our scope? Testing and certification reports for every cable?
- Cable tray, J-hooks, sleeves and pull strings for low voltage: whose scope?
- Access control, CCTV and intrusion: full systems or rough-in only? Who wires door hardware?
- Intercom, nurse call, paging, AV, or in-building radio coverage for emergency responders: specified?
- Incoming telecom conduits from the property line: ours or the carrier's?
- Coordination with the owner's IT or security vendor (patching, programming): defined?

## Mechanical & equipment connections

- Motor starters, VFDs and disconnects: supplied by mechanical or by us?
- Control wiring (building automation, 120 V controls, interlocks): mechanical's or ours?
- Every item on the mechanical equipment schedule has a power connection in our price?
- Voltage and phase on the mechanical schedule match the electrical drawings?
- Kitchen equipment and owner equipment connections: list complete?
- Elevators: feeders, disconnects, and pit and machine room lights and receptacles: included?
- Door operators, overhead doors, gates and barrier arms: power and controls carried?
- Water heaters, pumps, sump pumps and other plumbing equipment: connections counted?

## EV, generators, solar, storage

- EV chargers: supply, install, network setup, subscriptions and commissioning: whose scope?
- EV-ready means conduit and boxes only, or full circuits? Load management system required?
- Generator: manufacturer start-up, load bank test, first fuel fill, transfer switch: all carried?
- Generator pad, enclosure, exhaust and sound requirements: whose scope?
- Solar PV: utility connection approval, rapid shutdown, roof penetrations and structural review?
- Battery storage: utility approval, fire separation and ventilation requirements: identified?

## Demolition & existing conditions

- Extent of electrical demolition and make-safe: clear from the documents?
- Disposal of fluorescent lamps, old ballasts, batteries and other hazardous waste: carried?
- Designated substances (e.g. asbestos) where we cut or drill: is there a survey, and who abates?
- Tracing unknown circuits and updating panel directories in existing areas: allowed for?
- Shutdowns of the existing service: after hours, utility involvement, generator rental?
- Existing equipment to be reused: who tests it, and who pays if it fails?

## Testing, commissioning, as-builts, O&M manuals, training

- Insulation resistance, torque and infrared testing: specified?
- Third-party acceptance testing of switchgear or protective relays: required?
- Commissioning agent meetings and witness testing hours: allowed for?
- As-built drawings: marked-up prints or CAD files?
- O&M manuals: how many copies, and in what format?
- Owner training: how many sessions or hours, and is recording required?

## Coordination with other trades

- Coordination drawings or BIM clash detection: are we required to take part?
- Cutting, patching and painting after our work: ours or others'?
- Trenching, backfill and reinstatement for underground services: ours or civil's?
- Roof penetrations, curbs and flashing for electrical: whose scope?
- Supports and steel for heavy electrical equipment: ours or miscellaneous metals?

## Schedule, access & working hours

- After-hours, night or weekend work required (premium time)?
- Phased construction: how many separate mobilizations?
- Occupied building: limits on noise, dust, shutdowns or working areas?
- Schedule length realistic for the crew we priced? Any acceleration clause?
- Winter work: heat, hoarding, snow clearing, frost protection for underground work?
- Site access limits: loading dock or elevator booking, hoisting windows, delivery hours?

## Commercial terms

- Bid bond, performance bond and labour and material payment bond: required, and is the cost in?
- Holdback (10% under Ontario's Construction Act): release timing, and the cash flow it costs us?
- Prompt payment and adjudication terms in the contract: anything unusual?
- Warranty longer than the standard period, or extended manufacturer warranties: required?
- Insurance limits, wrap-up policy, additional insureds: can we meet them, and is the cost in?
- Liquidated damages: amount and what triggers them?
- Price escalation clause for wire, cable, switchgear and other volatile materials: is there one?
- Bid open for acceptance (irrevocable) longer than our supplier quotes are valid?
- Cash allowances and contingencies: included, and are they inside or outside our price?
- Separate prices, alternates and unit prices requested on the bid form: all answered?
- Supplementary conditions that change the standard CCDC contract (indemnity, back-charges, payment terms)?
